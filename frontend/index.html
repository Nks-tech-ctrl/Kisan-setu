The Operational Challenge

Procurement Shouldn't Mean Uncertain Waiting.

Lack of coordination and zero real-time visibility turn agricultural harvest intake into an unpredictable ordeal for farmers and facility operators alike.

1. Long and Uncertain Waiting Times

Without scheduled arrival slots, hundreds of trolleys arrive simultaneously. Farmers endure unpredictable overnight waits, fatigue, and days lost waiting for their turn.

Impact: Prolonged and uncertain waiting times during peak harvest season

2. Limited Visibility into Center Load

Farmers have zero advance intelligence on queue lengths or active capacity at specific centers, often choosing heavily clogged facilities while nearby centers sit underutilized.

Impact: Unbalanced center loads & unnecessary transit delays

3. Inefficient Manual Queue Management

Paper registers, physical token slips, and manual gate entries create administrative bottlenecks, line disputes, and lack of verifiable audit trails.

Impact: Gate disputes, human errors & lack of auditability

Queue Load Balancing Intelligence

Make Better Procurement Decisions

KisanSetu aggregates operational metrics across nearby facilities to provide data-driven recommendations. Instead of blindly joining long queues, farmers can compare centers across five critical operational parameters:

  • Distance & Transit Time: Distance from your farm to each authorized center.
  • Current Center Load: Real-time categorization (Low / Medium / High).
  • Estimated Waiting Time: Calculated based on vehicle queue and active weighbridge velocity.
  • Slot & Commodity Availability: Verified quotas for your specific harvest variety.
Operational Note: Data-driven recommendations help balance vehicle arrival rates across district centers, smoothing peak queue surges and reducing unnecessary waiting.
